# 047 — Row 2: narrower Agents card, apt-temps widget beside it

Proposal korg:4852 covers WI 4848 (the apt-temps widget) and WI 4849 (the
narrower Agents card). It is slice 2 of program korg:4855 and ran as a karc
leg (kpidash-b0ab3c) on kai under an overseer. Branch
`047-row2-apttemps-widget`.

## Goal

Ken, 2026-11-20. With `argus` joining the footer strip and the Altair and
PDP-8 lamp panels worth their space, the three apt-temps cards leave the
strip. The Agents card gives up about one host column, keeping its left edge,
and one apt-temps widget fills the gap: Living, Kitchen and Bedroom top to
bottom, with a green border when an unknown sensor is publishing.

## Premise check

- **WI 4849 holds.** The Agents card was 2520 wide (`AG_CARD_W`) in a 2528
  frame. The now line was at 2492, which is 13.6 px a minute over the 3-hour
  detail area.
- **WI 4848 holds.** One 220 × 240 card per `kpidash:apttemps:*` key, painted
  last in the footer strip with a move-to-last walk. apt-temps needs no
  change.
- **Width drifted, same direction.** The WI says about 379 px of cell. The
  host row's real pitch is 361 + 14 = 375 px (`HC_CW` + `HC_GAP`), so I took
  376 (see Decisions).
- kpidash is not in the cross-project-planning routing table.
- The preflight was clean, and the karc lock is the leg's own.

## Decisions

- **The split lives in `layout.h`**: `ROW2_APT_W` 376, `ROW2_AGENTS_W`
  2152, `ROW2_APT_X`. 376 is the host pitch rounded up to an even content
  width (368). Each cell carries its own `CELL_PAD`, so the gap either side
  of the widget is the 8 px every other pair of cards has. `test_layout`
  asserts that the split ends at `COL_X(4)`. `AG_FRAME_W` and `AG_CARD_W`
  are now derived from it, not literals.
- **The Agents plot keeps its 36 px right margin**: `AG_PLOT_X1` is
  `AG_FRAME_W - 36` = 2116. That makes 2080 px for 3 hours, or 11.6 px a
  minute. `AG_DETAIL_S` is untouched.
- **The legend moved from x 1500 to 1470.** At 1500 it would have run past
  the frame. At 1470 it ends at the now line, and the notes still have
  1000–1470.
- **test_agstate's pixel expectations were re-derived, not copied.** Each
  number comes from its time arithmetic at 693.33 px an hour (strip: 86.67).
  Four label-fit scenarios had their times nudged so they still exercise the
  same rule at the new scale:
  - the cut-to-20 overseer moved from 12:30 to 12:33;
  - "k-homelab" inside-cut runs 9.5 min, not 8;
  - the 31-character pair starts at 10:57, and its wide bar is 0.47 h;
  - the left-edge cut ends at 10:14.

  Each comment says what changed.
- **A pure model, `atstate`**, holds the zone table and the view. That puts
  the zone order, missing and stale zones, and the unknown-sensor rule under
  `just check` (`test_atstate`). The widget only draws it.
- **Zone names are the widget's own** ("Living", "Kitchen", "Bedroom"), not
  the payload's `zone`. The WI fixes the three, and a fixed label keeps the
  shape.
- **Unknown sensor**: a valid entry whose slug is not one of the three, and
  whose reading is under 300 s old (`APTTEMPS_FRESH_SECONDS`, the same
  window that greys a zone). It is never drawn. A stale one does not count,
  and evicting the key drops it from the registry at once.
- **The temperature uses the 96 px digits face**, as the calendar's clock
  and the weather filler do. That face had no full stop, so I added U+002E
  to it in `fonts/generate.sh` and regenerated. The unchanged face first
  regenerated byte-for-byte, which proved the toolchain reproduces it, and
  `check-fonts` now asserts the full stop.
- **The degree sign carries no "F"**: "72.4°". The 96 px face is digits
  only, and every reading on the panel is Fahrenheit.
- **The border**: no border normally, like the other row-2 cards. A fresh
  unknown sensor draws a 4 px green one, as two opaque fills (a green
  rounded rect with the base inset), not a border blend.
- **The registry's apt-temps entries lost their LVGL handles.**
  `apttemps_registry_remove` returns `bool`, and the evict path in `ui.c`
  only drops the entry for `kind: apttemps`. The footer's apt-temps block is
  gone. The stale and update cards' move-to-last walk is unchanged, and
  their comments now say `[services][stale][update]`.
- **The mock-up is LVGL renders, not an HTML page.** kai has no rasteriser
  (sprint 038's note), and the renders are the panel's own pixels. The render
  tool `render_apt_temps` doubles as a fit test. Its output is
  `sprints/planning/row2-047.png` and `apt-temps-states-047.png`, both
  attached to WI 4848 (`img-12fa`, `img-12fb`). The row render is on WI 4849
  too (`img-12fc`), with the crowded and long-name Agents states
  (`img-12fd`).
