# 049 — Usage card with Codex, and the Codex marker on the Agents Timeline

Proposal korg:4950 covers WI 4941, in program korg:4951 (Codex as a
first-class homelab agent). The sprint ran as a karc leg (kpidash-b57269) on
kai under an overseer, on branch `049-codex-usage-provider-marker`. Ken's
decisions are comments 5112 (usage card) and 5113 (timeline) on WI 4940; the
overseer's brief is comment 5120 on the proposal.

## Goal

Show Codex on the panel the way the mockup session decided: the limits card
becomes a "Usage" card with three rings in a triangle (Claude 7 day, Claude
Fable, Codex 7 day), and a Codex bar on the Agents Timeline carries a
Codex-green dot on a black patch.

## Premise check

- **Holds.** `codex:limits` was live on rpi53 at 13:50 PST (`source
  app-server`, `plan_type prolite`, `five_hour_none 1`, `seven_day_pct 1`),
  and `kdash:agents` carried 55 bars, 8 with `provider: codex`.
- **Drifted, in the direction the brief expected:** libkdash had no reader for
  either. kdashdata 029 contracted `codex:limits` (CD-33) and `provider` (CD-31
  addendum) code-free, and the addendum said outright that "the existing C
  reader ignores the field". WI 4941 asks for the parse "in libkdash beside
  `kdash_parse_claude_limits`", so the readers were written there first — see
  Cross-repo changes.
- kpidash is not in the cross-project-planning routing table.
- The preflight was clean; the karc lock is the leg's own.

## Decisions

- **The readers live in libkdash** (kdashdata sprint 030, PR kenhia/kdashdata#31):
  `kdash_parse_codex_limits()` / `kdash_codex_limits_stale()`,
  `KDASH_KEY_CODEX_LIMITS`, and `kdash_agent_bar_t.provider`. kpidash's
  `lib/kdashdata` pin moves to that branch's head; the ship turn repoints it to
  the squash-merge commit.
- **Triangle geometry.** Three rings of r 80 (stroke 22) where two were r 106:
  top centres at x 172 and 460, y 176; the bottom ring at x 316, y 414, just
  clear of the top rings' reset lines, which it overlaps horizontally. The
  96 px digits no longer fit inside a ring, so the number moved to a new
  **72 px bold digits face** (`fonts/lv_font_montserrat_bold_72_digits.c`,
  digits only, generated by `fonts/generate.sh`) with a 28 px "%". At 100 %
  the "%" grazes the arc, as "100%" did on the old card; left as is.
- **Every slot is fixed.** A ring whose feed has never published is not
  drawn; nothing moves when it appears. The card draws once either feed has
  published (`lim_has_any`).
- **The 5-hour mode is removed, not kept dormant.** Ken: no 5-hour display on
  this card, whatever a vendor publishes. With that, nothing in the layout
  depends on `five_hour_none`, so `lim_mode_t`, `lim_mode_of()`, `lim_mode()`,
  the three-record debounce (`LIM_MODE_STREAK`), the scoped bar and its label
  helper (`lim_fmt_scoped_label`) were deleted with their tests. `lim_observe()`
  lost its `now` argument, which only the debounce used. The `five_hour_none`
  fixture stays in `test_limstate.c` to prove such a record still parses.
- **Codex colour at high usage.** Codex green (`#1D9E75`) below 75 %, then the
  same yellow and red as the Claude rings at the same thresholds (75 / 90) —
  the overseer's reading in comment 5120, shown in the `warn` and
  `codex-high` renders for Ken.
- **Fable paces on the seven-day clock** (`LIM_SEVEN_DAY_S`), as the deck does.
  Its reset line drops when it is within 2 h of Claude's 7-day reset
  (`lim_resets_agree`, `LIM_RESET_AGREE_S`).
- **Stale, never 0 %.** Codex is held like Claude: an absent key or a rejected
  record keeps the last one, which greys by `updated_at` plus its cadence plus
  60 s. The header says which: "no data 22m" (Claude, unchanged) or "codex: no
  data 22m". The title greys only when every ring drawn is stale.
- **Ring labels**: "CLAUDE · 7 DAY", the scoped model's own ("FABLE · 7
  DAY"), "CODEX · 7 DAY".
- **The Codex marker**: an 18 px Crust patch, radius 5, 8 px in from the bar's
  outer left edge, with a 10 px Codex-green dot. A Codex bar's inside name
  starts 20 px further right (`AG_CODEX_LABEL_DX`), budgeted in agstate's
  `name_forms` before it chooses inside / outside / cut.
- **A bar narrower than the patch** (the WI asked for a proposal): a Codex bar
  is never drawn narrower than `AG_CODEX_MIN_W` (34 px), the same treatment
  every bar already gets at `AG_BAR_MIN_W` (16 px). The marker is never
  dropped. Shown in the `codex` render (the `korg` bar). Ken can rule
  otherwise on the image.
- **Legend**: a fifth item, "codex", with the marker itself as its swatch; the
  item gap went from 78 to 64 and `LEGEND_X` from 1470 to 1380 to keep the
  legend short of the now line.
- WI 5050 (long waits inside a bar) is untouched; the marker sits at the left
  end, so it has nothing to collide with there.
