What changes between the four views

  • Lanes: one row per host, as in the 12-hour card. Host names take your colours: kai peach, kubs0 green, cleo blue, other mauve.
  • Border colour: no lanes. Each bar carries its host's colour as a border, and bars pack upward into as few rows as fit. A working session is filled solid in its host colour, a finished one is an outline, and one waiting on you is yellow inside its border.
  • Links, with lanes: a line runs from the overseer on cleo to the start of each leg on kai or kubs0. Lines to kai cross the kubs0 lane.
  • Links, with border colour: the legs sit directly under their overseer, hung from it by short stems. With no lanes in the way, a program reads as one group.
  • Session names, hosts and times are made up for the picture. Two overseen programs are shown: one overnight, one late morning.
