kpidash refresh

Host cards and dev graphs, redrawn in the look the 10G and NAS cards set. Every card is drawn at real panel pixels (one cell is 632×628) with the fonts and sizes the repo already generates.

Numbers for kai, kubs0, cleo, rpi53, kubsdb and kimac are from this morning's panel capture. gratch, krgate, komarchy, thrall and kit are placeholders, there to show what ten or eleven hosts look like. GPU bursts in the graphs are invented so the thick line has something to do.

Today each host takes a full cell and two thirds of it is drive bars. All five variants shrink drives to thin bars that only change colour when a volume is filling up, and move RAM off red so red only ever means trouble.

Dev graphs

Today each graph is two cells wide and VRAM and RAM spend most of that space as flat lines. A is the same chart with a real thick line. B to E stop treating levels as traces.

The thick line is achievable

The LVGL in lib/lvgl tags every chart line segment with its series index, and its own chart examples restyle segments from a LV_EVENT_DRAW_TASK_ADDED callback. Setting the line width there per series gives a true 5 px GPU line and lets the six flanking series go. Not yet tried on the Pi; at 300 points the cost should be lower than today's nine series.
