The four cards

The same in all four

  • One accent per card: lavender for korg, sapphire for klams, peach for Claude. Green, yellow and red keep their usual meaning and appear only for health.
  • Peach numbers on korg mean "yours". The Awaiting Ken count turns peach when it is above zero, as the pool drives do on the NAS card.
  • Stale greys the card and says how old the data is.
  • Nothing under 20 px, and every fill is an opaque pre-mixed colour.
  • Fonts: the big numbers use the 96 px digits font added for weather. Everything else is a size the dashboard already has.

What each card needs that does not exist yet

korg and klams have no feed on the dashboard's Redis

Each needs a small publisher and a contract in kdashdata, the way weather got one. For korg, one board read gives every number here. For klams, the earlier plan (korg WI 826, cut on 9 November as not worth a 2×1) named klams-view as the publisher and that still holds.

Two history charts need someone to keep the history

korg's 30-day open count and klams's new-chunks-per-day bars are not stored anywhere today. The publisher would keep a short daily series in its own record. Without that, both cards still work and the chart areas go to something else.

klams: "last wrote" is not "last ran"

Today's real numbers show the catch. kubs0's scanner ran 28 minutes before I read it, yet klams has it last writing three days ago. The card can only show the write time unless the scanner's own timer is also reported. I have not found out why it has written nothing since the 10th.

Claude limits is the cheap one

The claude:limits record is already on the Redis the dashboard reads, and the shared reader library already parses it. The white pace tick is computed on the panel from the reset time.
