Weather in the host row, three ways

Drawn from kdash-weather.schema.json and nothing else: current conditions, today's four sun times and the daily forecast. Each candidate is shown where it would sit today, right of the seven host columns, and then at every width from four columns down to one.

To see it at the size it will be on the dashboard

Open this page on cleo's monitor 2, press Actual pixels, and set the browser zoom to 50%.

How the space works out

  • The row holds ten host-width columns. A host column is 361 px wide on a 375 px pitch, and ten fit across the panel.
  • Weather takes what the hosts leave, up to four. Seven hosts today leaves three. Eight leaves two, nine leaves one, ten leaves none and the weather is not drawn.
  • With six hosts or fewer the four-column version is centred in what is left. Your formula is right: the left pad is half a column pitch for each host under six.

The same in all three

  • Colours: yellow is the sun, peach is a high, blue is a low or rain. Nothing here uses teal, mauve or green, which already mean something on the panel.
  • A missing value is a dash. The schema sends null when a provider has no reading. "No current reading" shows that case.
  • Stale after an hour without a new record, as the schema suggests: the card goes grey and says how old the data is.
  • Nothing under 20 px.

Things to decide while you look

The big temperature needs a new font size

It is drawn at 96 px. The dashboard's largest font today is 48 px bold. A digits-only 96 px font (0-9, minus, degree sign, dash) is small to add.

The icons have to be built

Nine conditions plus a moon. They are drawn here from circles, lines and one polygon so they can be made as small image files or drawn directly. Either way it is new work on the dashboard side.

What two columns should show

At two columns there is room for the current conditions and one more thing. A shows three forecast days and drops the sun; B and C keep both in a smaller form.
