The card today, then four candidates

The switches above change every candidate at once. The 10G card and today's NAS card are screen captures, so they do not change.

The same in all four

  • No green border. The frame is a thin neutral line, as on the 10G card. It turns yellow or red only when something is wrong, which is the rule the host columns use.
  • One colour for "healthy". A healthy disk, a healthy pool bar and the SMART mark take the card's colour, the way teal means healthy on the 10G card. Standby is grey. Yellow and red keep their meaning.
  • The headline says the state. "NAS OK" where the 10G card says "ALL 10G". When something is wrong the headline names it.
  • Nothing under 20 px. Today's card uses 16 px for every detail line.
  • The activity chart is gone. gratch's CPU and RAM are now in its host column in row 1.

Things to decide while you look

Which colour

Lavender is the quietest next to teal and is not used for any reading elsewhere on the panel. Peach is the warmest contrast and is what the card's title uses today, but it is also the GPU colour in the host columns and sits close to warning yellow. Mauve is the RAM colour in the host columns.

How much disk detail

A and C drop the long model strings to fit a tile ("990 EVO Plus", not "Samsung SSD 990 EVO Plus 4TB"). That needs a shortening rule in the dashboard, like the one for OS names. B and D have room for a little more of the name.

The 10G card's own small text

Its labels are 14 and 16 px. Beside a NAS card at 20 px and up they will look smaller than they do now. Bringing the 10G card up to the same floor would be a separate sprint.
