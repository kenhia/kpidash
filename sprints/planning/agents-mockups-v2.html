Agents card, when sessions are short

The first mock-up drew sessions that ran for hours. Real ones run 10 to 30 minutes, which is about 25 pixels on a 24-hour axis: too narrow for a name. This page uses a day shaped like today's and shows two ways out.

To see it at the size it will be on the dashboard

Open this page on cleo's monitor 2, press Actual pixels, and set the browser zoom to 50%.

What each view does

  • As built: what is on the panel now, with this day's data. Only the long overseer bars can hold a name.
  • A · names beside bars: still 24 hours. A name that does not fit inside its bar is written to its left, in the host's colour, and the row packing leaves room for it. Names push bars onto more rows, and long session titles are cut at 20 characters.
  • B · recent hours + day strip: the main area shows only the last few hours, so a 15-minute leg is 100 to 200 pixels wide and holds its name. Under it, a strip shows the whole 24 hours with one thin lane per host and no names. The lighter band on the strip marks the hours shown above.
  • In both A and B a name goes inside the bar when it fits and to the left when it does not.
  • Session names, hosts and times are made up, but the lengths are today's real ones.
