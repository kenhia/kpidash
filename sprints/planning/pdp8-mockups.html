PDP-8/I front panel, for the space after the status cards

The strip is the bottom 276 pixels of the 3840-wide panel at real size. A small PDP-8 emulator runs the program you pick; each lamp's brightness is the share of machine cycles its bit was set since the last redraw. The five register rows are as on the real panel; the right-hand block is rearranged to fit 240 pixels of height.
